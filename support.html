Controls, purchases, saves, and how to reach us.

How to play

Hold a star or other body to bend around it. Let go when the verdigris spear crosses the ring.

With nothing pointed at, a hold takes the body you are flying toward. Touch with a second finger to tighten your turn. You can also play with a game controller.

Restore a purchase

Act One (four routes) is free. One purchase, The full heist, unlocks all sixteen routes across four acts.

To restore a purchase on a new or reset device, open Settings from the title screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Saved progress

Cleared routes, best scores, fastest times, marks, and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your saved progress.

Contact

For help or bug reports, email rashidproductions@gmail.com. If something went wrong, include your device model and iOS version.
