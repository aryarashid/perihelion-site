Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-23.

No data collected

Perihelion collects no personal data and no usage data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The game makes no network requests of its own. Purchases go through the App Store.

On your device

Your progress, best scores, marks, and settings stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase, The full heist, is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your name, billing address, or payment details.
